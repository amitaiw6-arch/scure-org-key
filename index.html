CSV scure-organization.csv

The file went to Downloads, not the drive?
  1. Open the Files app and find scure-organization.csv (Downloads).
  2. Long-press it, choose Move (or Copy), then pick the USB drive.
  3. On Android Chrome you can also turn on Settings → Downloads → Ask where to save files.

3 On the sCure Box

  1. Plug the USB drive into the sCure Box.
  2. Open Settings and find Organization.
  3. Tap Link Organization, then choose the CSV file from the drive.
