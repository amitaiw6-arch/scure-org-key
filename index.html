Origin signs you in with Google or Microsoft only on its own site, so this app uses a one-time bookmark that reads your organization there and brings it back here.

    4. 
        
  4. Tap the Get Org ID bookmark. Android Chrome: type Get Org ID in the address bar and pick the bookmark. iPhone Safari: open Bookmarks.

        What does the bookmark do?
        
It runs on the Origin page you are signed in to, in your own browser. It reads the organization from that page, or asks Origin's own server (api.origin.io - the only place your sign-in is ever used) for it, and then returns here with just the organization ID. Nothing is stored or sent anywhere else. The code:
